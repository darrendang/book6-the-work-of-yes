# 1. PURPOSE OF BOOK 6

Book 6 is a developing book about **decision-making**.

Its current governing question is:

> **What does someone need to see—or understand—before they have earned enough confidence to decide?**

Its current core principle is:

> **Earn the decision.**

Yes and no are outcomes. The deeper work is to reach a decision with enough perspective, inquiry, imagination, evidence, and judgment to act with justified confidence in either direction.

Protect the current Avoidance Principle:

> **The easy no avoids the work of possibility.**

> **The easy yes avoids the work of judgment.**

> **Do not use a decision to avoid the work the decision requires.**

Book 6 must preserve the tension between possibility and judgment rather than becoming a simplistic doctrine of persistence, optimism, saying yes, or refusing no.
