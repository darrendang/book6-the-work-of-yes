## Provisional decision anatomy

**Widen the lens → Expand alternatives → Test assumptions → Examine consequences → Judge → Decide**

This is a hypothesis for finding recurring decision movements. It does not prescribe one sequence for every decision.

The approved sufficiency question provides a possible closure condition: would further inquiry materially improve or change the decision? This must remain conditional and evidence-aware.

## Candidate techniques to test

- Change stakeholder perspectives.
- Change time horizons.
- Zoom in and out.
- Use inversion.
- Examine extremes.
- Look for contrary evidence.
- Test assumptions experimentally.
- Compare consequences explicitly.

Stories should demonstrate when a technique helped, when it did not, and what it cost. A method list is not evidence of an earned framework.

## Developmental authority — working dimensions

Where bounded responsibility is appropriate, investigate:

- stakes and severity of consequences;
- reversibility;
- demonstrated judgment;
- truthfulness and reliability;
- understanding of consequences;
- ability to recover from error;
- whether the downside is survivable.

Working cycle:

**bounded responsibility → observe judgment → experience consequences → learn → widen or tighten authority**

Keep this as a developmental lens. It must not become a universal formula for parenting, delegation, human worth, or intrinsic agency.
