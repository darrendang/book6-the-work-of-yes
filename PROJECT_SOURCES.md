## 8. Story mining and provenance

For a new Book 6 decision story, preserve:

- who was involved;
- what decision actually existed;
- what was known at the time;
- what constraints were real versus assumed;
- what evidence was available;
- what alternatives were considered;
- what happened next, if known;
- what remains memory or interpretation;

- whether the story supports, qualifies, or contradicts the emerging intellectual spine.

Do not convert hindsight into contemporaneous knowledge.

Do not turn a compelling example into independent corroboration of a general principle.
