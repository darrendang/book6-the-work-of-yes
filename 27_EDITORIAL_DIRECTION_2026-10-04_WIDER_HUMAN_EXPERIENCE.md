# Book 6 — Editorial Direction: Familiar Ground, Wider Human Experience

**Status:** APPROVED EDITORIAL DEVELOPMENT DIRECTION  
**Approved:** 2026-10-04 Pacific  
**Authority:** Darren's explicit instruction and confirmation in the October 4 voice conversation.  
**Scope:** Narrative emphasis and story-development selection. This is not new conceptual canon or a final architecture.

## Approved direction

Ground Book 6 in the thinking returning readers may recognize, then expand outward through lessons and decision stories from other people. Reader familiarity is an intended bridge, not an established audience fact or a prerequisite for understanding the book.

Retain the governing question and **Earn the decision**. Give other people's decisions greater narrative weight and develop their experiences in their own right. Darren's experience remains a selective thread rather than the default carrier of every movement.

## Development implications

- Develop interview accounts around specific choices, contemporaneous knowledge, competing evidence, consequences, and turning points.
- Let accounts support, qualify, or challenge the emerging framework. Do not force interviews into the five movements.
- Familiar examples should earn reuse by contributing a materially new decision insight rather than repeating prior-book lessons.
- Preserve other people's perspective, uncertainty, and agency. Do not turn them into supporting characters whose purpose is to validate the author.
- Frameworks and perspectives should emerge through examination of choices; author synthesis must remain distinct from source testimony.
- No fixed proportion of author stories versus interview stories is established.

## Boundaries and provenance

This public-safe record preserves only the approved editorial direction. It does not disclose private interview contents, assign named people to manuscript roles, authorize contact or publication, or claim additional interviews have happened.

Specific narrative carriers, follow-up interview plans, exact quotations, source verification, permission, and final placement require their own development. The conversation discussed possible follow-up interviews; those remain proposals.

The five-movement architecture remains provisional. Final title, complete manuscript, production, publication, and Book 6's formal relationship to The Way remain open at their existing scope.
