## Approved questions — original development order

1. Tell me about a consequential decision where your first answer changed—or became harder to defend. What did you initially see, and what did you discover that challenged it?

2. Take me back to a choice you made while important things were still unknown. How did you judge whether to act, wait, or investigate further? What remained unresolved when you chose?

3. Tell me about a decision where people disagreed about what should happen. How did you work out whose choice it was, whose knowledge mattered, and what responsibility belonged to you?

4. Tell me about a decision whose outcome made you question your original reasoning. Looking only at what you knew beforehand, how do you now assess the choice—and what did you do with what you learned afterward?

5. Tell me about a decision where someone else helped you see something important you had missed. What did they understand that you didn’t, and how did that change—or strengthen—your choice?

## Use

Ask one question at a time and invite a specific decision. Let the person tell the story before introducing Book 6's framework. Distinguish what was known at the time from later interpretation. Preserve contrary evidence, unchanged choices, and unresolved outcomes.

The guide is for story mining with colleagues and other interviewees. It authorizes no outreach or publication of their answers. Detailed testimony remains governed private evidence under PROJECT_SOURCES.md.
