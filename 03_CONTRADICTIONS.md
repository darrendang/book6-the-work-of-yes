# Book 6 — Contradictions and Failure Cases

Purpose: actively test and potentially break the thesis rather than collecting only confirming examples.

Mine cases where working toward yes becomes:
- unsafe
- unethical
- legally impermissible
- fiscally irresponsible
- operationally wasteful
- scope creep
- avoidance of a necessary decision
- pressure to violate sound professional judgment
- inability to set legitimate boundaries

## Core challenge

The book must not become “always find a way” or “never take no for an answer.”

A legitimate no may be the responsible outcome. The test is whether the no has been earned through sufficient inquiry, judgment, and respect for the relevant guardrails.
